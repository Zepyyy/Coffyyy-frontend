# Sonnet revision review

Owner requested Sonnet 5.5 after rejecting the lead's previous acceptance decisions.
Four scene implementors plus an independent read-only Sonnet critic. Current scope:
Nocturne, Leaffall, Passionflower and Orbital. Daylight retired; source preserved.

## Independent critique received

Critic inspected baseline source and captured stills, not motion or in-progress
revisions. Its motion observations are source-derived and need rendered confirmation.

Main finding: scenes were arranged around spare UI margins rather than their
subjects' physical setting and relationships. Texture and coverage were mistaken
for composition; captures selected active moments and hid long empty intervals.
Daylight's orange plane lacked a readable receiving surface, while Aurora at least
represented a coherent luminous phenomenon. Aurora was not rejected by the owner.

## Lead's review gates

These are scene-specific evidence requirements, not a recipe for every background.

- Watch continuous sequences, including quiet intervals and event transitions.
  Inspect populated desktop at 1440×900 and 1920×1080, both themes.
- Nocturne: varied overlapping activity and meaningful setting between events;
  no identical isolated streak replay. Preserve moon relief and meteor material.
- Leaffall: water readable before impact; leaf actually descends from its node;
  reflected anatomy, contact and ripples share one surface; meaningful float/drift.
- Orbital: preserve admired moon/transit/planet materials; added or retained elements
  have clear orbital roles and depth relationships, rather than mere pixel coverage.
- Passionflower: preserve petal shading and butterfly; moderate related botanical
  enrichment, convincing connected anatomy and visible travel. No jungle.
- Technical checks remain necessary but cannot establish visual acceptance.

The critic proposed specific meteor counts, a common radiant, exact added-element
counts and placements. Those are suggestions, not owner requirements. Assess the
result's coherence and interest rather than prescribing them universally.

Implementation submissions and continuous rendered reviews pending.
